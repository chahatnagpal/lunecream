Our Studio Philosophy

Where Sculpture Meets Haute Pâtisserie

Founded in 2021 in the historic cast-iron district of Soho, Maison Sucre was conceived as an antidote to mass-produced bakery confectionery.

We view cake design as a tactile medium: delicate layers of slow-fermented chiffon, house-made fruit preserves, and velvet-textured Swiss meringue buttercream, crowned with hand-pressed edible botanicals and architectural ruffles.

We source our butter directly from Normandy, our vanilla beans from smallholder Tahitian farms, and our edible florals from certified organic Northeast gardens.

Éléonore de Saint-Germain
Chef & Creative Director

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know regarding cake sizing, lead times, delivery, and dietary accommodations.

For signature collection cakes from our catalogue, we require a minimum of 48 hours notice as all sponges and buttercreams are made fresh to order. For bespoke multi-tiered wedding cakes, we recommend booking 4 to 8 weeks in advance.

We provide complimentary studio pickup from our Soho studio (148 Mercer St, NY) during business hours, as well as white-glove temperature-controlled courier delivery across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, and the greater Tri-State area.

Cakes should be kept refrigerated until 1 to 2 hours before serving. Buttercream cakes are at their absolute best texture and flavor when enjoyed at room temperature.

Yes, we offer gluten-free adaptations and eggless recipes for several of our signature cakes. Please note that while we take strict care, our kitchen handles wheat, nuts, and dairy products.
